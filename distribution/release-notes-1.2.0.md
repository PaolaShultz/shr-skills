# Fructal Cap Design 1.2.0

The contract is 42% shorter: 1,104 words, down from 1,914. All six cap questions
are unchanged. The canonical skill, ChatGPT demo, and host packages are synchronized.

- Activation now requires a concrete connection between a retained constraint
  and obstructed progress; a small defect can qualify, and declining the method
  does not abandon the task.
- Review permits related recommendations without specifying a full replacement.
  Mixed requests such as "review and fix" are distinguished from explicit mode limits.
- Authorized sensitive or rate-limited reads are allowed within their limits.
- Missing authority permits a useful conditional handoff. Cancellation accounts
  for partial work and operations already in flight.
- Unknown cap answers remain uncertainty. Every Redesign includes a concrete
  verification plan. Mode-label and attribution ceremonies are removed.
- Evidence claims, external-outcome reconciliation, and untouched-state checks
  are explicit. The test harness no longer treats mode labels as correctness.
- Harness checks cover nested state and permissions, require returned content
  for skill inspection, retain full command results, and reject the known marker
  and encoded-read bypasses. A separate judge model can be selected.

The retained 1.1.1 evaluations remain historical evidence. They do not validate
this revision. Regression tests and model checks establish only their tested
scope, not cross-model reliability, superiority, or affected-user acceptance.

See the [1.2.0 regression record](../docs/evaluations/live-contract/fructal-1.2.0/)
for the initial failures and focused rechecks. These are development cases,
not a held-out benchmark.

Validation: deterministic package, harness, distribution, and evidence-check
regressions passed. The 29-case development matrix exposed five initial
failures; all five passed after the documented corrections and focused rechecks.
Eight final-skill runs passed. This was not a second full-matrix run.
