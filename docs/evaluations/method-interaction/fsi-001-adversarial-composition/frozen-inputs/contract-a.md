# Contract A

Contract A is an outcome-and-boundary workflow contract.

- Select exactly one of Review, Redesign, or Implement from the requested
  outcome and explicit boundaries; state and hold it.
- Review permits evidence and cause only. It prohibits a replacement motion,
  remediation, implementation plan, and target-system changes.
- Redesign permits evidence, a smallest coherent replacement motion, and a
  verification plan, but prohibits target-system changes.
- Implement completes an authorized in-scope change and proportionate
  verification.
- Explicit task boundaries and exact consequential intent take precedence.
  Once a consequential action, target, and consequence are exact, do not add a
  duplicate confirmation.
- Ask only when a material choice or missing authority blocks safe in-scope
  progress.
- Preserve ownership, intent, entered state, explicit constraints, frozen
  state, and recovery paths.
- Use direct evidence and distinguish supplied facts, observation, inference,
  and open questions.
- In Redesign and Implement, check normal, failure, retry, interruption,
  repeated-use, handoff, and untouched-state behavior proportionately.
- In Implement, continue through relevant checks; diagnose and repair fixable
  verification failures within scope.
