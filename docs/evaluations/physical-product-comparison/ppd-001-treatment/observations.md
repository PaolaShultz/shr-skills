# Treatment observations

These are unscored treatment observations. No control artifact was inspected
and no comparative conclusion was made.

## Tool and source observations

- Both required Fructal Cap Design files passed the SHA-256 gate before their
  bodies were read.
- The browser transport returned cache-miss errors for the four immutable raw
  SHR-DAW GitHub URLs. Direct `curl` retrieval of those same frozen URLs
  succeeded.
- One combined workflow-skill read was truncated by the shell output limit.
  The relevant skill files were re-read completely in bounded reads before
  design work continued.
- The exact model identifier, reasoning level, underlying image model,
  generation seed, sampling parameters, quality, and requested output format
  were not exposed.
- Exactly one substantive image-generation request completed successfully. No
  transport retry or aesthetic correction generation occurred.
- A system-required memory pass was performed before product-source research.
  It exposed repository routing, public naming, installed/source validation,
  push policy, and non-enclosure SHR-DAW integration notes. It did not expose a
  control artifact, another enclosure design, the protocol rubric, or the
  protocol amendment.

## Generated-image observations

- The board contains the requested exterior, airflow cutaway, and
  connectors/service views, and it includes the requested concept-only footer.
- The exterior is recognizably a restrained dark wedge with an inclined
  display, broad front grille, and low rear body. It avoids knobs, RGB styling,
  people, and unrelated front controls.
- The cutaway depicts the display above the Pi, local cooler, lower NVMe
  assembly, broad front inlet, split upper/lower blue airflow, and rear exhaust
  fan. Component geometry, spacing, port positions, and flow arrows are
  illustrative and must not be treated as measured evidence.
- The connector view depicts four USB ports and Ethernet together, plus three
  smaller adjacent ports. The smaller shapes do not unambiguously prove one
  USB-C and two micro-HDMI connectors, and their panel reads more rear-facing
  than the selected right-rear side bay.
- The image does not show attached external cables or demonstrate rearward bend
  radius, strain relief, cable-pull stability, or actual direct alignment with
  the rotated physical stack.
- The labelled NVMe hatch is drawn primarily as a lower rear panel rather than
  an unambiguous underside/bottom access hatch.
- The service callouts suggest removable modules but do not prove fastener
  retention, service loops, keyed plugs, independent removal order, or access
  with real hands and tools.
- Despite the prompt's request to keep text beyond the title, three panel
  labels, and footer absent, the image added several service callout labels and
  tiny pseudo-interface text on both displays. These were not corrected because
  aesthetic or content correction generations were prohibited.
- The three views are visually coherent but not a dimensional projection of one
  verified geometry. No exact measurements appear.
- The render provides no thermal, acoustic, electrical, RF/EMI, structural,
  accessibility, or manufacturability evidence.

## Later-noticed design concerns

- The written concept selects a right-rear power/display bay as an ergonomic
  assumption. Real cable handedness, collision, and stability remain open until
  the physical stack and representative plugs are measured.
- The divided airflow proposal depends on the actual Active Cooler direction,
  rear fan curve, leakage around connector inserts, and clearances around the
  bottom base. The image cannot resolve those dependencies.
- A removable coarse dust screen remains conditional because pressure loss,
  noise, and cleaning frequency were not measured.
- The proposed fan electrical route remains deliberately unresolved because
  the GPIO display wiring and power budget are unknown.

## Archival validation observations

- The first staged `git diff --check` reported one extra empty line at EOF in
  five new Markdown records, including the pre-generation design and image
  prompt containers. Those empty lines were removed after generation without
  changing any frozen prose or the fenced prompt text. Both the pre-generation
  and normalized design-response hashes are retained in the artifact manifest.
- The first credential-pattern scan used an inline case-insensitivity flag that
  the Git regex engine rejected. Its apparent pass message was not accepted as
  evidence; a compatible scan was run afterward.
