**Color palette.** The warm cream gives the dark navy headline a softer, more bookish feel than stark white would. Coral brings warmth and energy to that pairing. Its visual weight depends on how much appears in the poster. A small coral detail could enliven the page, while a broad patch could compete with the headline. The muted gray’s exact shade matters too. A pale gray could make the body text feel washed out against the cream.

**Typography.** The 54 pt serif headline and 12 pt sans-serif body establish a decisive hierarchy through both scale and letterform. The serif gives the title an editorial voice, while the sans-serif gives the body a quieter texture. At four and a half times the body size, the headline may feel like a separate piece of lettering. If the two feel disconnected in print, a slightly larger body size would narrow that gap while preserving the title’s prominence.
