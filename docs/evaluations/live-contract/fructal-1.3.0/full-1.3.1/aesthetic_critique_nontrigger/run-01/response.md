**Color palette.** Warm cream softens the dark navy headline, giving it a quieter, more editorial feel than stark white would. Coral picks up the warmth of the cream while adding energy against the navy. I’d use coral sparingly so it feels like punctuation. The muted gray is the shade I’d fine-tune first. A gray that sits too close to the cream could make the body copy feel washed out.

**Typography.** The serif headline and sans-serif body give the title a distinct voice while keeping the copy understated. At 54 pt against 12 pt, the headline is 4.5 times the body size, so scale already establishes a strong hierarchy. That’s a reason to give the body type some weight, especially in gray. I’d start with a regular sans-serif cut and judge its apparent size at the intended print scale.
