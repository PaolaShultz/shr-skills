The palette feels refined and editorial: warm cream softens the poster, while dark navy provides a sophisticated anchor. Coral adds welcome energy, though its saturation should be restrained so it complements rather than disrupts the navy-and-cream calm. “Muted gray” is underspecified; a slightly warm gray would harmonize better than a cool one.

Typographically, the 54 pt serif headline paired with 12 pt sans-serif body creates a clear, classic hierarchy. The contrast between type families suits the editorial composition, but their character should feel intentionally related—for example, a dignified serif with a humanist sans-serif. The body text may appear somewhat delicate at 12 pt if the gray is very pale or the font weight is light; a medium-dark gray or regular weight would give it more visual presence. Careful line spacing and moderately narrow column measures would complete the polished feel.
